A little about me

I'm Sadia, a Software Engineering student at NUML in Islamabad with a 3.8 CGPA and a soft spot for interfaces that feel simple and kind to use. Since 2023 I've been building responsive web apps with React.js, JavaScript and modern tooling, and I love taking a project all the way from first idea to a live link.

Along the way I've explored a few corners of tech: a frontend internship at Decode Labs, C/C++ debugging at Codveda Technologies, email marketing at Soplex Technologies, and running a Shopify store as a junior e-commerce manager. Each one showed me another side of how products reach real people.

I'm happiest when I'm working with a team, untangling a stubborn bug, or learning something new. Lately that has meant machine learning, from a house price predictor to AI courses on my certificate wall.
